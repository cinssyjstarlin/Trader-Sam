About Us

Who We Are

PT Gabungan Samudera Internasional has been sourcing, processing, and exporting premium Indonesian seafood since 2014.

Where it began

Founded at Muara Angke, 2014

PT Gabungan Samudera Internasional was founded in 2014 at Muara Angke — Jakarta's oldest working fishing port. The idea was straightforward: Indonesia has some of the richest waters on earth, and the world deserved better access to what those waters produce.

The archipelago spans over 17,000 islands with year-round species diversity. Yet for decades, much of that supply never reached international buyers in a form they could rely on — consistent quality, proper cold chain, documented compliance. That gap is what this company was built to close.

Starting with a single cold storage unit and a core group of fishermen partners, we grew into a trusted mid-scale exporter — shipping to 23 countries across Asia, the Middle East, and Oceania.

Leadership
Then Herry Yulianto
Founder & CEO — PT Gabungan Samudera Internasional

Mr. Herry Then grew up around Indonesia's coastal fishing communities, which gave him an early and practical understanding of the industry — the logistics, the informal networks, and the gap between what Indonesian waters produce and what actually reaches international markets in a reliable form.

In 2014, he founded PT Gabungan Samudera Internasional at Muara Angke with a clear mandate: build an export operation that international buyers could depend on. That meant investing in cold storage infrastructure before it was comfortable, obtaining HACCP, GMP, and Halal certifications that meet the requirements of Japan, South Korea, and the Middle East, and building direct relationships with fishermen rather than relying on intermediaries.

"Indonesia is the largest maritime nation in Southeast Asia. The potential is enormous — but potential alone doesn't create value. What creates value is discipline, standards, and long-term relationships."

— Then Herry Yulianto, 2020

Under his leadership, the company became one of Indonesia's largest squid exporters by 2018. When COVID-19 disrupted global supply chains in 2020, PT Gabungan Samudera Internasional maintained exports above 500 tonnes per month — because the systems had been built correctly in advance. The company now exports to 23 countries across Asia, the Middle East, and Oceania.

Track record

Ten Years of Progress

2014

Founded at Muara Angke

Export operations begin. First cold storage unit commissioned. Initial focus on squid and demersal species to regional markets.

2016

International certifications achieved

HACCP, GMP, and Halal certifications obtained — opening access to Japanese, South Korean, and Middle Eastern buyers with strict import compliance requirements.

2018

Indonesia's 2nd largest squid exporter

Consistent supply chain management and growing fishermen networks establish the company as one of Indonesia's leading cephalopod exporters.

2020

New cold storage — stable through COVID-19

Expanded processing capacity commissioned. Despite global disruptions, the company maintains 500+ tonnes monthly exports — a milestone noted at the facility inauguration by government officials.

2024

Strategic partnership with Perum BULOG

A formal supply collaboration with Indonesia's national food logistics agency formalizes the company's role in the national seafood supply chain.

2026 Now

Expanding product range and export reach

Active export operations across 23 countries. FDA registration current. Ongoing development of processed and value-added product lines for European and Gulf markets.
